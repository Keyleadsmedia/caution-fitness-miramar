Programs

Nine Ways To Train.

One membership, one facility, and enough range that your program can change without you having to change gyms.

See Class Times Start Free Trial

CrossFit

A high-intensity program that blends elements from several sports and training styles into constantly varied workouts. Every session is coached and every movement is scalable, so a first-week beginner and a competitive athlete can train side by side.

Seven days a week

HYROX & Endurance

Built around the HYROX race format: running intervals paired with functional workout stations. Conditioning work for anyone chasing a race, a time, or a genuinely harder engine.

Mondays & Wednesdays, 5:30 PM

All Glutes & Glory

A dedicated lower-body strength block. Programmed separately from the daily class so posterior chain work gets real, focused volume rather than an afterthought.

Mon & Wed 7:30 PM · Fri 5:30 PM · Sat 8:30 AM

CrossFit Kids

Strength, coordination and conditioning scaled for younger athletes. The emphasis is on movement quality, confidence and having a good time under a coach's eye.

Mon, Wed & Thu, 5:30 PM

Personal Training

One-on-one and private small-group coaching for athletes who want programming written around their own goals, schedule or injury history. Packages run twelve sessions per month.

By appointment

Strongman

Maximum-weight barbell and implement work. For lifters who want to find out what their actual ceiling is, coached so the load never outruns the technique.

Coached sessions

Olympic Weightlifting

Snatch, clean and jerk, and the positions underneath them, taught in a dedicated lifting area by certified coaches.

Dedicated platform area

Nutrition Coaching

A 45-minute session covering BMI, body fat percentage and endurance testing, then a plan that fits how you actually eat and train.

45-minute sessions

Homebody Fitness

A structured at-home program for travel weeks, recovery weeks, or any stretch where getting to the gym is not realistic.

Train anywhere

Not Sure Where To Start?

Most people begin in the daily CrossFit class, because every movement in it scales. Your coach adjusts the load, the range of motion and the pace to whatever you can do safely on day one, then moves it as you improve.
