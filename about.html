About

A Gym Built For Work.

13,000 square feet on Miramar Parkway, coaching nine programs across two South Florida locations.

Who We Are

Caution CrossFit & Fitness is a training facility on Miramar Parkway serving Miramar and Pembroke Pines, with a second location in Miami Lakes.

The floor is set up for range rather than a single specialty. CrossFit runs seven days a week as the backbone, with HYROX, strongman, Olympic weightlifting, a dedicated lower-body class and a kids program built around it.

Every class is led by a professionally trained and certified coach. That is the part that does not scale down: whatever program you are in, someone is watching your movement and adjusting it.

How We Coach

Three things that hold true in every class on the schedule.

Scale First

The workout is written once and adjusted for every athlete in the room. Load, range and pace all move to meet you.

Technique Before Load

Weight goes up when the movement earns it. That is the whole reason the Olympic lifting area is coached rather than open.

Train The Whole Week

Nutrition coaching and the at-home program exist because what happens outside the gym decides most of the result.
