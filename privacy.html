Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and what happens to it.

Information We Collect

This site collects only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, the program you are interested in, your training experience, and anything you write in the message field.

The site does not use advertising trackers, does not sell data, and does not build a profile of you across other websites.

How We Use It

Form submissions are used for one purpose: to reply to your enquiry, schedule a trial or class, and answer questions about training. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

How It Is Handled

The site is hosted on Netlify, and form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify's form handling service on our behalf. Netlify's own privacy practices govern that processing.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies, including web beacons and tracking pixels, to run this site, understand how it is used, and measure marketing. Depending on what is enabled for this site, that can include:

  • Google Analytics 4 — measures how visitors use the site. Google describes its handling of this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Meta Pixel — measures the results of advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and can be used to show ads to people who have visited this site. See Meta’s Privacy Policy.
  • Metricool — measures site traffic alongside our social media reporting.

These tools may set cookies in your browser and share information with the providers named above. We do not sell your personal information.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. You can opt out of Google Analytics using Google’s browser add-on, and control ad personalisation in your Meta account under Ad Preferences or at optout.aboutads.info. Blocking cookies may stop parts of this site working.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log the request as part of delivering the font files.

Your Choices

You can ask us to delete anything you have submitted through the form. Contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

Children

The contact form is intended for adults. Enquiries about the youth program should be submitted by a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change with it.
